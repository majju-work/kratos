6-Month Data Scientist Course Roadmap

Transform aspiring data professionals into corporate-ready data scientists with this hands-on, industry-fueled roadmap. Each stage builds technical depth, business acumen, and production-readiness.

Months 1-3 Months 4-5

Month 6: Corporate-Grade Capstone Projects (12 Full-Scale Builds)

Wrap up with a sprint of end-to-end projects tackling real business needs—portfolio-ready evidence for job interviews.

💥 Capstone Projects Lineup

  • Customer Lifetime Value Prediction: Build and deploy a model predicting high-value customers from transactional data.
  • Product Recommendation Engine: Implement collaborative filtering or content-based recommendations for e-commerce.
  • End-to-End A/B Testing Platform: Design, execute, analyze, and visualize business experiments.
  • Credit Risk Scoring Model: Deploy models to predict default with full EDA, feature selection, and reporting.
  • Time Series Forecasting Platform: Automate sales/demand forecasting with multiple models and scenario analysis.
  • Social Media Insights & Sentiment Analysis: Create an NLP-driven dashboard for real-time campaign monitoring.
  • Real-Time Fraud Detection System: Build a pipeline for streaming anomaly detection (Spark/Dask optional).
  • Churn Prediction for SaaS/Telecom: Develop an end-to-end solution including deployment and scheduled retraining.
  • Interactive Executive Dashboard: Build with Tableau, Power BI, or Streamlit for live business metric monitoring.
  • Image Classification/Recognition Engine: An end-to-end deep learning project with automated data labeling & reporting.
  • Business Data Lake Integration: Automate ingest, transformation, and querying from disparate sources.
  • Explainable AI Reporting Suite: Generate reports with SHAP/LIME explanations for model outcomes.

🎓 Learning Experience & Outcomes

  • Every lesson includes hands-on code and immediate mini-projects.
  • Team collaboration through peer reviews, pair programming, and code demos.
  • Frequent business case studies provide real-world context.
  • Become an expert in essential and advanced data science techniques.
  • Gain fluency in production workflows and business problem-solving.
  • Graduate with a portfolio of practical, interview-winning projects.
